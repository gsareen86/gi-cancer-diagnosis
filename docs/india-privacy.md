# India privacy applicability and production readiness

As of **2026-09-04**. Scope: GI Compass's planned India patient service and its operators, clinicians and processors. This is an engineering/legal-readiness register, not legal advice, certification or a claim of DPDP, SPDI or HIPAA compliance. An Indian legal adviser must confirm applicability to the actual operating entity, contracts and deployment before production.

[Directions, clauses (i)–(iv)](https://www.cert-in.org.in/PDF/CERT-In_Directions_70B_28.04.2022.pdf). |

The commencement instruments use elapsed periods, not fixed future calendar dates. Gazette issues are dated 13 November 2025; MeitY lists the documents on 14 November. Use November 2026/May 2027 as planning milestones, have counsel record the precise effective dates, and recheck before each phase. The December 2025 [corrigendum, G.S.R. 892(E)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/12/3c7ebbae0e5456f493f486e6845df86b.pdf) corrects wording, including Rule 1's publication reference, without changing its one-year/eighteen-month intervals. Do not use the January 2025 draft as the operative Rules.

The DPDP Act has no separate statutory “sensitive health data” category. Future readiness includes purpose-specific processing, notices, demonstrable consent and comparable-ease withdrawal, processor contracts, access/correction/erasure, grievance and nomination rights, and child-data safeguards. A child is under 18; retain the project's adults-only scope until a separately approved minor/guardian workflow exists. Assess Significant Data Fiduciary duties if designated; do not assume that status merely because this is a health application. [DPDP Act, sections 2–14](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf).

Future Rule 7 requires affected-person and initial Board notices without delay, followed by detailed Board information within 72 hours of awareness, unless the Board permits longer. It does not replace CERT-In's six-hour clock. Rules 6 and 8(3) introduce one-year retention requirements that must be reconciled with clinical/legal retention and erasure. [Final Rules 6–8](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf). CERT-In permits initial reporting with available information and later supplements; do not wait for a completed investigation. [CERT-In FAQ 30](https://www.cert-in.org.in/PDF/FAQs_on_CyberSecurityDirections_May2022.pdf).

## Residency: an explicit project policy

GI Compass requires approved India locations for production patient-data processing, databases, uploads, model inference, logs and backups. This is a product/deployment constraint, **not a blanket DPDP localisation mandate**: section 16 provides for notified transfer restrictions and preserves stricter applicable protections. [DPDP Act, section 16](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf). Current SPDI Rule 7 imposes transfer conditions; CERT-In has a distinct Indian-jurisdiction ICT-log requirement. [SPDI Rule 7](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/15063), [CERT-In clause (iv)](https://www.cert-in.org.in/PDF/CERT-In_Directions_70B_28.04.2022.pdf).

Project controls must cover vendors, subprocessors, remote support, crash reporting, email/SMS and recovery copies—not just the database region. Use notification links without clinical content or medical attachments; document unavoidable recipient metadata flows. The current local llama.cpp provider is a deployment choice, not proof of production residency. No silent hosted-model fallback, external patient-data training or new processor without privacy/security approval.

## Production evidence gates

All gates below are **OPEN: production evidence not assessed in this documentation task**. Repository code, synthetic tests, a local model and UI sign-off buttons do not close them. Role owners below must be assigned to named people. Store dated evidence and approval references in a restricted release register, never patient records or credentials in this document.

| Gate / accountable role | Evidence required before admitting production patient data |
| --- | --- |
| P1 — Legal/privacy lead | Signed applicability assessment identifying operator/clinic roles, current versus future duties, SPDI transition, applicable clinical-record rules, and exact commencement dates. Recheck notifications, exemptions and any Significant Data Fiduciary designation. |
| P2 — Product/privacy lead | Approved data/purpose inventory and notices; versioned consent receipts; demonstrated withdrawal propagation; published grievance contact; authenticated request handling for access, correction, erasure and nomination. Record lawful retention exceptions and the supported language/age scope. |
| P3 — Infrastructure/vendor owner | Region and data-flow evidence for every store, inference endpoint, backup and processor; executed processing/security contracts; subprocessor/support-access inventory; egress controls and a tested no-cloud-fallback failure path. |
| P4 — Security lead | Independent assessment and remediation evidence covering role/assignment isolation, MFA, session revocation, signed uploads/downloads, encryption/key custody, least privilege, tamper-resistant audit trails, redacted telemetry and restore drills. |
| P5 — Records/privacy owner | Approved retention matrix by data class and legal purpose, reconciling current and future minima; tested deletion, processor propagation, backup expiry and documented clinical/legal holds. No blanket “delete everything on withdrawal” promise. |
| P6 — Incident commander | Staffed escalation, CERT-In contact/reporting procedure, six-hour exercise, time-sync evidence and India log retention/retrieval test. Prepare the separate future DPDP notices and 72-hour follow-up workflow with timestamped rehearsals. |
| P7 — Clinical lead | Verified RMP authority and clinical/regulatory assessment for the intended service. Manual physician prescriptions and editable AI drafts require physician review and explicit release; autonomous prescribing/diagnosis and raw AI patient output remain prohibited. |
| P8 — Release owner | Named approvals, linked evidence, unresolved-risk decisions and a legal-change review date. Reassess before production, before each commencement milestone, and after material provider/purpose/deployment changes. |

Future DPDP controls are an early project readiness target; that does not make them current statutory duties. This register does not establish medical-device clearance, telemedicine eligibility, ABDM certification or HIPAA applicability. Those determinations require separate review of the actual service.
