The patient UI shows a persistent text placeholder when an asset cannot load. It never silently removes a “See examples” result. Uploaded patient reports remain in private case storage and are never reused in the reference library.

Before activation, record exact file/version, hash, source URL, author, licence, attribution copy, changes, clinical reviewer, review date, approved locale and next review date. Keep captions usable without images. Do not derive a diagnosis from apparent skin/stool colour in a phone image.
